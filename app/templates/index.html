Lynis Documentation & Compliance

What is Lynis?

Lynis is an open-source security auditing tool for systems running Linux, macOS, or Unix-based OS. It performs extensive health and security scans.

Regulatory & Compliance Mapping

Lynislens maps automated Lynis findings directly against industry benchmarks:

  • CIS Benchmarks: Hardening profiles for SSH, authentication, logging, and sysctl parameters.
  • NIST CSF: Categorization into Identify, Protect, Detect, and Respond controls.
  • PCI-DSS 4.0: Firewall enforcement, vulnerability patch status, and access control.
